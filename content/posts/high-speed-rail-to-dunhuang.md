# Why Take the High-Speed Rail?
Traveling across the Gobi Desert at 250km/h is an experience in itself. 

## 1. How to Book Tickets
For foreign travelers, we highly recommend using **Trip.com**. It supports international credit cards and provides English service. 

## 2. What to Bring
* **Your Passport:** This is your ticket. You don't need a paper ticket anymore.
* **Alipay:** For buying snacks on the train.

## 3. The Route
Lanzhou West -> Zhangye West -> Jiayuguan South -> Dunhuang.
